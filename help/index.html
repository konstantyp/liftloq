How to use Liftloq

Local workout calendar · No account required
Screens shown are design comps matching current UI (1.2.x); device photos may replace them later.

Liftloq keeps your workout plan on your device. Mark days on the calendar, manage workout types, and optionally track supplement spending — without signing in.

1. Mark a workout on the calendar

  1. Open Calendar (home).
  2. Tap a day → pick a workout type (up to two per day when supported).
  3. The day cell shows the type color and label.

2. Add and remove workout types

  1. From Calendar, tap the Workout types button (FAB), or use Manage types on a day screen.
  2. Add: tap the add (+) control → enter a name and pick a color.
  3. Remove: delete a type from the list (workouts of that type leave the calendar).

3. Track supplements (Supps money pit)

  1. Open the navigation drawer → Supps money pit.
  2. Add a purchase (name, price, date). Currency comes from Settings.
  3. See Total spent on supplements and the Money burnt list.
Tip: Swipe left on Calendar to cycle Calendar → Stats → Supps money pit (then back to Calendar).
