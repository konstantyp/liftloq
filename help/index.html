How to use Liftloq

Local workout calendar · No account required · Updated for version 2.1.4

Liftloq keeps your workout plan on your device. Mark days on the calendar, manage workout types, plan and time HYROX workouts, and optionally track supplement spending — without signing in.

1. Mark a workout on the calendar

  1. Open Calendar (home).
  2. Tap a day (or the add button). The day sheet has collapsible Bodybuilding, HYROX workouts and Notes sections.
  3. Pick up to three entries in total (Bodybuilding types and HYROX workouts combined). At 3, the other picks are disabled until you deselect one.
  4. Tap Save. Nothing is stored until you save.
  5. The day shows one colour band per entry: Bodybuilding first, then HYROX.

2. Add and remove Bodybuilding types

  1. Open the menu (☰) → Bodybuilding. Or on a day sheet, tap Manage types.
  2. To add: tap +, enter a name, pick a color, then tap Save.
  3. To remove: tap the trash icon on a type, then confirm Delete.

3. Track supplements (Supps money pit)

  1. Open the menu (☰) → Supps money pit.
  2. Tap + to add a purchase (name, price, date), then tap Save.
  3. Set currency under Settings (same menu).
  4. See Total spent on supplements and the Money burnt list.

4. HYROX β

  1. Builder: menu (☰) → HYROX (beta) → +. Name the workout, pick a colour and division, then tap + Full race for the 16 official segments or + Add segment to pick a run or station from the list (sorted into Runs and Stations). A new segment opens ready to edit (distance or reps, weight); tap any card to open it, use ⋮ → Move up / Move down to reorder, and the red trash to delete it (you confirm first). The list scrolls; Cancel and Save stay at the bottom.
  2. Delete: on the HYROX list, tap the red trash on a workout (or Delete inside the editor). Confirm — sessions are removed and the workout is cleared from every calendar day. Official race is not re-seeded after you delete it.
  3. Plan it: on a day sheet, open HYROX workouts and pick one or more, then Save.
  4. Start HYROX: on a day with a planned HYROX workout, a Start HYROX button appears above the add button. With several planned, it opens a menu to choose one.
  5. Race mode: tap Split at the end of each segment. Pause freezes the clock (paused time is not counted), Resume continues, the undo button removes the last split. Finish (top bar) asks for confirmation. Then review and Save; results show with an OK button back to the calendar.
  6. Stats: Stats → HYROX β shows History (tap a session for its results), best splits and station decay.

5. Settings: sections and toggles

  1. Menu (☰) → Settings. Sections: General (language, currency, motivation quote), Modes, Workout plan and About.
  2. Modes: turn Bodybuilding or HYROX (beta) off to hide everything for that mode (calendar entries, legend, stats, menu pages). Nothing is deleted; turning it back on restores it. One of the two must stay on.
  3. A language change is applied when you tap Save (bottom right).

6. Export / import

  1. Settings → Workout plan → Export workout plan saves a JSON backup (types, days, notes, supplements and HYROX data).
  2. Import workout plan replaces your data from such a file, after a confirmation. Days with more than 3 entries keep the first 3.
Tip: Swipe left below the calendar to cycle Calendar → Bodybuilding → HYROX β → Stats → Supps money pit. Pages for a mode that is off are skipped. Settings stays in the menu only.
